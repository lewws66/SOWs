Algebra Quadratic Functions Completing the square, discriminant $b^2-4ac$ for roots & line-curve intersections, quadratic inequalities. Excludes definite quadratics Includes all G2 scope plus conditions for $ax^2+bx+c$ to be always positive ($a>0, D<0$) or always negative ($a<0, D<0$). Full Scope
Algebra Polynomials & Partial Fractions Remainder & Factor Theorems, cubic equations; partial fractions with distinct linear and repeated linear denominators only. Includes all G2 scope plus partial fractions with irreducible quadratic denominators $\frac{px+q}{(ax+b)(x^2+c^2)}$. G3 Extension
Algebra Surds Four operations on quadratic surds and simplifying $\sqrt{a^2b}$. Excludes conjugate rationalisation & equations Includes rationalising denominators via conjugate surds $\frac{c}{a \pm \sqrt{b}}$ and solving radical equations. G3 Extension
Algebra Exponential & Logarithmic Functions Base-10 and general base-$a$ ($a>0, a\neq 1$) indices & logarithms, laws of logs, change-of-base, graphs. Excludes $e^x$ & $\ln x$ Includes natural exponential function $e^x$, natural logarithm $\ln x$, and base-$e$ modeling. G3 Extension
Geometry Coordinate Geometry & Circles Parallel/perpendicular lines ($m_1m_2=-1$), rectilinear area, Linear Law (power models), and standard circle $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$. Includes general circle form $x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0$, tangents & normals to circles, and base-$e$ Linear Law. G3 Extension
Trigonometry Trig Functions, Identities & Equations Six trig ratios (acute), $\sin/\cos/\tan$ general angles & graphs, $\frac{\sin A}{\cos A}=\tan A$, $\sin^2 A+\cos^2 A=1$, basic equations. Includes reciprocal identities ($\sec, \csc, \cot$), addition & double-angle formulae, $R$-formula $R\cos(\theta \mp \alpha)$, and identity proofs. G3 Extension
Geometry Proofs in Plane Geometry Omitted from G2 (4051) Deductive geometric proofs using triangle congruence/similarity, Midpoint Theorem, and Alternate Segment Theorem. G3 Exclusive
Calculus Differentiation & Applications Algebraic functions $x^n$ & $(ax+b)^n$, product/quotient/chain rules, tangents/normals, rates of change, first derivative test for stationary points. Includes derivatives of $\sin, \cos, \tan, e^x, \ln x$ and second derivative test ($\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \gtrless 0$) for maxima/minima. G3 Extension
Calculus Integration & Kinematics Indefinite & definite integrals of $x^n$ & $(ax+b)^n$ ($n \neq -1$), area bounded by curve & $x$-axis, rectilinear kinematics (algebraic). Includes integrals of $e^{ax+b}, \frac{1}{ax+b}, \sin, \cos, \sec^2$, area bounded by $y$-axis, and transcendental kinematics. G3 Extension
